Privacy Policy

GeoLearn: Kids World Map • COPPA & Families Policy Compliant

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to GeoLearn: Kids World Map. We are deeply committed to protecting the privacy and safety of children and all users who use our educational application.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

GeoLearn does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses.
  • We do not collect precise geographic location or IP addresses.
  • We do not collect device advertising identifiers (AAID) or hardware serial numbers.

2. 100% Ad-Free Experience

GeoLearn contains zero third-party advertisements. There are no banner ads, interstitials, or video ads targeting children.

3. Local Storage Only

All game data and progress (including stars earned, visited countries, passport stamps, and mistake quiz review records) is stored exclusively on the user's local device using Android's private internal storage. This data never leaves your device.

4. COPPA & Google Play Families Policy Compliance

Our application is designed in strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families Policy.

  • No behavioral tracking or profiling is performed.
  • No third-party analytics SDKs are embedded in the application.
